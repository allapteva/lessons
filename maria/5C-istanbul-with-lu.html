          Too much, not enough

          How much? How many? More than you want, or less than you need? Listen to the examples and repeat them.

          • How many? Things you can countmany / a fewHow many bags? Just a few.
          • How much? Things you can’t countmuch / a littleHow much luggage? Only a little.
          • Botha lot ofI take a lot of shoes and a lot of luggage.

          Too and enough

          MeaningPatternExample
          more than you wanttoo much + can’t countI have too much luggage.
          more than you wanttoo many + can countThere are too many people at the gate.
          more than you wanttoo + adjectiveMy suitcase is too heavy.
          the right amountenough + nounDo we have enough water for Lu?
          the right amountadjective + enoughIs the carrier big enough?

            Travel tips

            Flying with a small dog

            On many airlines, a small dog can fly in the cabin with you. It travels in a soft carrier under the seat in front of you. The dog and the carrier together usually can’t weigh more than eight kilos.

            Book early. There are only a few places for pets on every flight, and sometimes there aren’t enough.

            Visit the vet a few weeks before the trip. You need the dog’s documents, and you can’t fly without them.

            Choose the right carrier. It can’t be too small: your dog needs to stand up and turn around. But it can’t be too big for the space under the seat.

            On the day, don’t give your dog too much food before the flight, but bring enough water. And bring a few treats for a good dog!

                  1. YouExcuse me, is the carrier small enough for the cabin?

                  Scene 1: check-in

                  You are at the airport with Lu. Your teacher is the check-in agent. Show your passport and Lu’s documents, ask about the carrier, your seat and your luggage, and get your boarding pass.

                  Scene 2: at the hotel

                  You arrive at your hotel in Istanbul. Your teacher is the receptionist. Ask about the room, a place to walk Lu, and the best café nearby.
