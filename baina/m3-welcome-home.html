            How many?

            When we count things in a house, we ask: How many… are there?

            Чтобы спросить, сколько чего-то в доме: How many… are there?

            The question

            How many bedrooms are there?

            После How many — слово во множественном числе: bedrooms, windows.

            The answer

            There are three.

            There is one.

            There aren’t any.

                  A typical English house

                  Read about a house in England. Then say: true or false?

                  Прочитай про обычный дом в Англии. Потом реши: правда или нет?

                  A typical English house

                  Many families in England live in a house with two floors. Downstairs, on the ground floor, there is a hall, a living room and a kitchen. Upstairs, on the first floor, there are two or three bedrooms and a bathroom. Yes, in England the first floor is upstairs!

                  There is a small garden in front of the house and a big garden behind it. Next to some houses there is a garage, and on the roof there is a chimney. English people love their gardens. There are flowers and trees, and there is a little house for the birds too.

                    🏡

                    Did you know?Some houses in England have a name, not only a number, like “Rose Cottage”.

                    Speaking

                    Come in!

                    A visitor comes to see a house. Listen to the conversation. Then read it with me: first you are the visitor, then you show the house. Then give me a tour of your home.

                    Гость пришёл посмотреть дом. Послушай разговор. Потом прочитаем его по ролям: сначала ты гость, потом ты показываешь дом. А потом проведи мне экскурсию по своему дому.

                    A shows the houseB is the visitor

                    1. AHello! Come in, please.
                    2. BThank you! What a lovely house!
                    3. AThis is the living room.
                    4. BIt’s very nice! How many bedrooms are there?
                    5. AThere are three. They’re upstairs.
                    6. BIs there a garden?
                    7. AYes, there is. It’s behind the house.
                    8. BCan I see the kitchen, please?
                    9. AOf course! It’s next to the living room.
