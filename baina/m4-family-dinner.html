My uncle Tom has got fair hair and a beard. My cousin Ben is five. He is short, of course! And me? I’ve got dark hair, like my mum and dad.

              Pronunciation

              She’s: is or has?

              In speaking, she is and she has both sound like she’s. Listen to the next word: got? Then it’s has. Listen and say. Then listen: is or has?

              В речи she is и she has звучат одинаково: she’s. Слушай, что дальше: если got — значит has. Послушай и повтори. Потом угадай: is или has?

              • She’s tall.she’s = she is
              • She’s got long hair.she’s got = she has got
              • He’s got a beard.
              • He hasn’t got a beard.
              • hair, fairодин долгий звук, r не читается

              Is or has?

              Нажми 🔊 и послушай. She’s / He’s — это is или has?

                🐻‍❄️

                Did you know?A polar bear has got white fur, but its skin is black!

                Grammar

                She is tall. She has got…

                Tall, short: use is. Hair, eyes, a beard, glasses: use has got.

                Рост — с is: She is tall. Волосы, глаза, борода, очки — с has got: She has got long hair.

                is / has got

                Grandpa is tall.

                He has got a beard.

                Ben hasn’t got a beard.

                Questions

                Has she got long hair? — Yes, she has. / No, she hasn’t.

                В вопросе has встаёт в начало, got остаётся после имени.
