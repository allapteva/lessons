      Halloween words

      Halloween is on Saturday! Tap a picture and listen. Say the word. Then choose the right word.

      Хэллоуин в субботу! Нажми на картинку, послушай и скажи слово. Потом выбери слово.

          Halloween in Britain

          Хэллоуин — 31 октября. Дети переодеваются в привидений и ведьм, стучат в двери и говорят «Сладость или гадость!». Им дают конфеты.

          • Halloween is on the thirty-first of October.
          • Children dress up as ghosts, witches and bats.
          • They knock on doors and say: Trick or treat!
          • People give them sweets.
          🦇

          Did you know?Bats sleep in the day and fly at night. They sleep upside down!

          Spooky th

          Remember: tongue between your teeth. A quiet th, like in three, and a buzzing th, like in this. Sort the words. Then say the spooky sentences like a ghost!

          Язык между зубами. Тихий звук (three) и звонкий (this). Потом скажи фразы голосом привидения!

          Say it like a ghost

          • There are three bats in the bathroom.
          • There is a ghost on the third floor.
          • This is the thirteenth pumpkin!
          • Thank you for the sweets!
          🕷️

          Did you know?A spider has got eight legs, and most spiders have got eight eyes!

          There is / There are

          We use these words to say what is in a place.

          Так мы говорим, что где находится: «В комнате есть…»

          One thing

          There is a bed.

          There is an ant farm.

          Один предмет: There is a…

          Two, three, four…

          There are two beds.

          There are five rooms.

          Несколько: There are… и -s в конце слова.

            Look and say

            Посмотри на картинку и скажи предложение. Потом нажми Check.

                Antville

                Read about Mila’s ant farm. Then say: true or false?

                Прочитай про муравьиную ферму Милы. Потом реши: правда или нет?

                Antville

                Hi! I’m Mila. I’m eleven. This is my ant farm. Its name is Antville. It is a house for ants, and it is in my bedroom.

                There are five rooms in Antville. The first room is the kitchen. There is food and water in it. The second room is the dining room. The ants eat there. The third room is very big. The queen lives in this room. There are many small white eggs in the fourth room. The fifth room is the garden. The ants walk there in the morning.

                  👂

                  Did you know?Ants haven’t got ears. They feel sounds with their legs.
