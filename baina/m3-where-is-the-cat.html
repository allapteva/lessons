Where is the cat?

A cosy autumn home. First your school homework. Then: where things are (in, on, under, behind…), whose things they are (my, your, his, her), Lily’s bedroom with an ant farm, and a cat who loves hiding. Take your time. Nothing here is on a clock.

Уютный осенний дом. Сначала школьная домашка. Потом: где что стоит (in, on, under, behind…), чьё это (my, your, his, her), комната Лили с муравьиной фермой и кошка, которая любит прятаться. Не спеши.

    School homework

    Your homework first

    Open your book and your diary. What is your homework? Write it here, then we do it step by step.

    Открой учебник и дневник. Запиши, что задали, и сделаем по шагам.

      Ask for help in English

      Нажми 🔊, послушай и повтори. Эти фразы пригодятся и в школе.

      • Can you help me, please?
      • I don’t understand this word.

      Warm-up

      Secret autumn houses

      It’s autumn, and the hedgehog and the squirrel are at home. What is in their houses? Ask a question first: “Is there a sofa?” Then tap and find out.

      Осень, ёжик и белка дома. Сначала спроси вслух «Is there a…?», потом нажми на карточку и узнай.

      3

      Vocabulary

      Where is the cat?

      Seven little words say where something is. Tap a picture and listen. Say the sentence. Then choose the right word.

      Семь маленьких слов говорят, где что находится. Нажми на картинку, послушай и повтори предложение. Потом выбери слово.

          🐿️

          Did you know?In autumn a squirrel hides thousands of nuts under the leaves. It forgets some of them, and new trees grow!

          4

          Pronunciation

          The beat of a sentence

          Some words have a loud part and a quiet part: behind. A sentence has a beat too: the cat is under the sofa. Listen, tap the beat on the table, and say it.

          В словах есть громкая и тихая часть: beHIND. У предложения есть ритм. Слушай, отстукивай ритм по столу и повторяй.

          Loud part, quiet part

          • behindbeHIND
          • betweenbeTWEEN
          • in front ofin FRONT of
          • underUNder
          • next toNEXT to

          Tap the beat

          • The cat is under the sofa.
          • My bed is next to the window.
          • The dog is behind the door.
          • Her books are on the desk.

          Where is the loud part?

          Нажми 🔊 и послушай слово. Какая часть громкая?

            🦉

            Did you know?An owl can turn its head almost all the way round, but it can’t move its eyes.

            Grammar

            Whose is it?

            My room, your room, his room, her room. Tap a card and listen. Then choose the right word.

            Мой, твой, его, её: чья это комната? Нажми на карточку и послушай. Потом выбери слово.

                🐜

                Did you know?An ant has two stomachs: one for its own food and one for food it shares with its friends.

                  Reading

                  Lily’s bedroom

                  Lily has an ant farm, like you. Read about her room. Then say: true or false?

                  У Лили, как и у тебя, есть муравьиная ферма. Прочитай про её комнату. Потом реши: правда или нет?

                  My bedroom

                  Hi! My name is Lily and I’m eleven. This is my bedroom. It’s small, but I love it. My bed is next to the window, and my desk is in front of the window. There is a lamp on the desk.

                  My books are on the bookcase, and my ant farm is on the bookcase too! There are two hundred ants in it. Their house is a big glass box. My cat Biscuit sleeps under my bed. Her favourite place is behind the door. And where is my dog? He is in the garden!

                    🐱

                    Did you know?A cat sleeps for about fifteen hours a day. Under a bed is a favourite place.
