      Spa day: one fan, one sceptic

      Olga has her nails done every two weeks and treats herself to a massage whenever work gets stressful. Her friend Mark, a keen cyclist, has never been to a spa in his life. ‘I’d rather spend the money on a new bike,’ he says. So when a new wellbeing centre opened near their office, we sent them both to try it for a day.

      They started with a hot-stone massage. Olga loved it: ‘I felt as if I’d slept for ten hours.’ Mark was less sure. ‘It was relaxing at first, but after forty minutes I couldn’t stop thinking about my emails.’

      Next, they had their faces cleaned and moisturised in a facial. Mark admitted that his skin had never looked better, but he didn’t enjoy lying still with cucumber slices on his eyes. ‘I kept wanting to scratch my nose,’ he said.

      The surprise of the day was the yoga class. Mark expected to find it boring, but he enjoyed it more than anything else. ‘My back hurts after long rides, and the stretches really helped. I’m thinking of joining a class.’ Olga, who usually does yoga at home, found the class too slow.

      Would they go back? Olga is already planning to book a whole day there for her birthday. Mark says he probably won’t have another facial, but he’d happily go back for the yoga, and maybe the massage, if he could leave his phone in the car.

            B. The passive, in every tense

            active: who did it matters

            Peter the Great founded the city in 1703.

            passive: the thing matters

            The city was founded in 1703.

            present simple
            The castle is visited by thousands of people every year.
            present continuous
            The cathedral is being restored at the moment.
            past simple
            The palace was built in the eighteenth century.
            past continuous
            The bridge was being repaired when we visited.
            present perfect
            The tower has been used as a prison and a museum.
            past perfect
            The old church had been destroyed by fire, so a new one was built.
            future
            The new museum will be opened next spring. It is going to be opened by the mayor.
            with a modal
            Photos can’t be taken inside the crypt. Tickets must be booked online.
            infinitive, -ing
            The abbey had to be rebuilt after the fire. I love being shown round a city.
            who did it
            Say by + person only when it matters: The church was designed by Gaudí. But: My bag was stolen. (we don’t know who)
