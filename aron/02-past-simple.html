EVENT 6 · POWER CUBES

-ed spelling

Regular verbs in the past: add -ed. But watch these four rules!

Most verbs: + ed

play → played
watch → watched

Ends in -e: + d

live → lived
dance → danced

Consonant + y: -ied

study → studied
try → tried

But vowel + y: stay → stayed

One syllable, one vowel + one consonant: double it

stop → stopped
chat → chatted

But two vowels: clean → cleaned. Never double w or y: show → showed

EVENT 7 · SOUND CHECK

-ed sounds: /t/, /d/ or /ɪd/?

Click a word (you'll hear it), say it, then click the right box.

Only after a t or d sound do we say an extra syllable: wanted /ɪd/, needed /ɪd/. Everything else stays short: watched /t/, played /d/. After p, k, s, sh, ch and f sounds we say /t/: stopped, walked, danced, washed. After other sounds we say /d/: lived, played, studied. Put your hand on your throat: /d/ buzzes, /t/ doesn't.

My crazy Saturday

by Rosie Wilson

Hi everyone! Yesterday was Saturday, and it was a crazy day. Remember last week? The pool was closed. Well, yesterday it was open again, so I was there at six o'clock. I trained for an hour. Then it started to rain, and I walked home in the rain. I was so wet!

Dan was at home all morning. His computer was OK again, so he played computer games for four hours. Mum wasn't very happy about that!

In the afternoon we visited our grandparents. Grandma baked a chocolate cake, and Grandpa showed us some funny old photos of Dad. Nora danced in the living room, and we all laughed a lot.

Sara and Fatima weren't with me. They were at the cinema in town. They watched a scary film and posted about twenty selfies. Typical!

In the evening I was really tired, so I stayed at home and watched TV with Dad. I was asleep at nine o'clock!
