The best match ever

In the afternoon I played Brawl Stars with my friends Max and Leo. The first match was terrible. We lost! So we started again. I played Colt, and I shot the red team's Bull three times. EVENT 3 · TRAINING CAVE

didn't: one past is enough

How do we say that something didn't happen?

It happened

Danwonthe match.
Weplayedchess.

The past is in the verb: won, played.

It didn't happen

Dandidn't winthe match.
Wedidn't playchess.

The past is in did. didn't = did not.

One past is enough

did + not + win

did is already the past, so the verb goes back to normal.

The same for everyone

I / you / he / she / we / they didn't go

No -s, no -ed, no went after didn't.

Both teams

played → didn't play
went → didn't go

Rule-followers and rule-breakers work the same way.

Don't say didn't won → didn't windidn't went → didn't godidn't played → didn't play
  • We lost the first match. We didn't win.
  • Rosie went to the pool. She didn't go to school.
  • Dan watched a video. He didn't watch a film.
